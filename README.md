# About my Favorite Series, The Boxer

![The Boxer](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/the_boxer/images/d/d5/The_Boxer.png/revision/latest?cb=20200611180544)

**The Boxer** is a South Korean webtoon (manhwa) written and illustrated by Jung Ji-Hoon (also known as JH)

### Basic Information
• **Genre:** Action, Drama, Sports, Psychological

• **Original Run:** December 15, 2019 – June 16, 2022 (Naver Webtoon)

• **English Publisher:** [LINE Webtoon](https://www.webtoons.com/en/sports/the-boxer/list?title_no=2027) (digital) and [Yen Press / Ize Press](https://yenpress.com/) (print volumes)

• **Volumes:** 11 collected volumes

• **Anime Adaptation:** An anime television series adaptation produced by Breccia and Studio Jemi (directed by Takehiro Kubota and Won Yeong Kang) has been announced to stream on [Crunchyroll](https://www.crunchyroll.com/)

### Plot & Premise
• **The Protagonist:** The story centers on Yu, a 17-year-old former shut-in who suffered severe bullying and possesses supernatural, god-given reflexes and an eerie, emotionless talent for dodging punches.

• **Coach K:** A legendary veteran boxing trainer who has previously created five world champions discovers Yu and tries to mold him into the ultimate fighting machine

• **Core Theme:** Rather than just a standard sports tournament arc, the story uses boxing as a psychological and spiritual deep-dive, heavily focusing on the tragic backstories, motivations, and internal struggles of various opponents and rivals.
